# 8 Queens Puzzle Rules

1. The board has 8 rows and 8 columns.
2. Place exactly 8 queens on the board.
3. No two queens may share the same row.
4. No two queens may share the same column.
5. No two queens may share the same diagonal.
6. A valid solution satisfies all rules at the same time.

The puzzle has 92 distinct solutions when rotations and reflections are counted as separate board arrangements.
