# Dino Runner Game Rules

- Use Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to move the dinosaur.
- Press Space, Up, or W to jump.
- Avoid the cactus obstacles. A collision ends the run.
- The score increases while the game is running, and the obstacle speed gradually increases.
- Press Restart or R to start a new run. Space also restarts after Game Over.
- The original 8 Queens puzzle remains available from the main window.
